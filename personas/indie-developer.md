## CONSTRAINTS
- Validation must include a paying customer or strong pre-order signal — don't build for months without revenue signal
- [GATE_DATE] is the hard gate — scope all product plans to prove income before then
- Use AI-native stack where possible (Claude API, Vercel, Supabase) — fastest path to ship
- One product in focus at a time — resist starting multiple MVPs simultaneously

## HANDOFF
Before switching away from indie-developer mode:
- Update `knowledge/indie/idea-vault.md` with any new ideas or validation results
- Update `knowledge/indie/revenue-log.md` with any income events

## GOTCHAS
- Idea validation ≠ product validation ≠ revenue validation — be precise about which stage you're at
- Don't spend more than 2 weeks on an idea without a monetization experiment
- AI tools make building easy but distribution is still hard — weight 60% effort on distribution, 40% on building
- Don't let security/bounty work bleed into indie dev time — they need separate focus blocks
- Content channel and digital product are complementary: channel builds audience, product captures revenue
